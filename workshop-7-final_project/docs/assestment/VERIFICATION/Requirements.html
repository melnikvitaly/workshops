 5 Надійність 4 / 4
26
 5.1 Використано WDT ✅ esp_task_wdt with ctrl and safety subscribed, ~1 s timeout. esp_reset_reason() logged at boot, WDT-reset counter kept in NVS, laser comes up off after any reset.
27
 5.2 Є обробка помилок периферії ✅ Four SD conditions (no card, removed while running, full, write error) each log once, flag the OLED and never stop the loop; mount retried on a slow timer. OLED missing or NAKing disables ui only. EYE reopens the serial port and rebuilds the camera pipeline instead of exiting.
28
 5.3 Передбачено перевірку некоректних даних ✅ 256-byte line cap → discard to the next newline and count it; CRC-8 on NDJSON lines (counted, not fatal); range checks on every field. Counters bad_crc, overlong, unparsed, out_of_range in telemetry and on the OLED.
29
 5.4 Система не зависає при помилковому вводі ✅ eye/tools/fuzz_link.py — truncated lines, 10 kB lines, NUL bytes, binary noise, NaN, 1e300, half a frame then reset, stale replays. Pass = counters climb, uptime does not reset, the gimbal never moves on garbage. E-stop stays out-of-band, accepted from any transport in any state.
